Anouk Journo

Author, Translator, Co-founder of L'AMagie

✦

Published author for more than thirty years, Anouk Journo is a novelist, poet, English translator, and certified animal mediation facilitator.

Author of about thirty works published by Bayard, Fleurus, Gallimard Jeunesse, Rustica, Talents Hauts, HarperCollins, Larousse… and translator of more than one hundred books from English.

After directing the Dixit association (writing workshops, life storytelling) for twenty years, she co-founded L’AMagie — associative, caring, and poetic publishing.

SGDL Member · Occitanie Culture Unique Agency · Félines-Minervois

I write, I translate, I teach, I accompany…

Writing Workshops & Coaching

Anouk has led over twenty years of creative writing workshops for all levels, in institutions and privately.

Five pathways of support:

  • Group writing workshops — collaborative and caring creation
  • Individual creative coaching — bring your project to life or move it forward
  • Editing support — professional texts, biographies, content
  • Therapeutic writing — Mots pour Maux — see below
  • Animal mediation — workshops where animal presence opens the conversation

Therapeutic Writing — Mots pour Maux

Individual online sessions to write, journey through, understand, and find freedom.

Writing is embarking on an inner voyage. Every word becomes a key, every sentence a passage.

Zoom sessions, caring and confidential space guaranteed by charter. First consultation free.

Single Session €50 / hour Duration flexible between 1 and 2 hours.
5-Session Package €200 Ongoing support, at your pace.

Animal Mediation — Certified

Trained in 2025 with Les Entrepreneurs Animaliers, certified. Also holds the ACACED "Dogs" credential issued by the French Ministry of Agriculture — essential for professional work with dogs.

Workshops combining animal presence, dialogue, and writing — for clients in socio-professional, educational, or artistic accompaniment.

Creation of personal or collective collections (memory, cognitive, artistic).
